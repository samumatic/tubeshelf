Channels you already follow are skipped, so importing twice is safe.

How to get your YouTube subscriptions
  1. Open Google Takeout and click Deselect all.
  2. Tick YouTube and YouTube Music, click All YouTube data included, and leave only subscriptions ticked.
  3. Create the export. Google emails a link, usually within minutes.
  4. Unzip it and choose Takeout/YouTube and YouTube Music/subscriptions/subscriptions.csv here.
